Care is going AI-enabled, and the bar for quality and compliance only keeps rising.

Most providers already trust their systems with funding and care minutes, but there's one problem sitting underneath all of it: risk is usually found out about after it's happened, not before.

The Pattern

A resident's mobility fades quietly. Until the fall that changes everything.

The decline is often invisible until the moment it isn't, and that moment is costly on every measure that matters: to the resident, to the family, and to the service. Today, most homes act after the fall, because there's rarely been a practical way to act before it.

Business As Usual

By the time it's written up, the moment to act has already passed.

It's the same loop in most facilities: something happens, the team documents it, and it's reviewed weeks or months later. The risk was sitting there long before anyone could act on it.
