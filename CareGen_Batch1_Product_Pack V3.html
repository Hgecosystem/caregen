Executive overview · for leadership

Five packages. One clinical problem each.

Batch 1 is five CareGen packages. Each pairs Talius sensing technology with an HG clinical service. We launch the Essential tier now; Support and Managed follow once the eCase care-system link is built.

How it works
1
Talius senses

Sensors capture falls, movement, vitals and call events - no cameras.

2
HG runs the clinical service

HG configures the alerts, keeps them current, monitors and reports - the service wrapped around the tech.

3
The client gets outcomes + evidence

Fewer incidents, and Commission-ready evidence of what was done, by whom, with what result.

Review & next step

HG leadership confirms the product set, tiering and fees; Talius confirms the technology and rates on the Package Detail tab. Once you've reviewed, please reply by email to confirm Batch 1 — or flag any changes — and we'll move to implementation.
