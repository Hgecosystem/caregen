Compliance & Trust

Built for Australian aged care.

CareGen is designed around the standards and privacy framework this sector actually operates under, not adapted from a US healthcare template.

Aged Care Quality Standards

Every CareGen deployment is configured against Australia's Aged Care Quality Standards, with reporting built to support Star Rating and Commission evidence, not a generic compliance checklist.

Australian data residency

Resident and facility data is hosted and stored within Australia, so it stays subject to Australian law and never has to leave the country to be processed.

Privacy Act 1988 (APPs) & consent

Data is handled under the Australian Privacy Principles, with resident and representative consent captured before monitoring begins and documented as part of the care record.

Cybersecurity & access controls

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with role-based access so staff only see what's relevant to their role, and every access and change is logged.

No cameras. No audio.

CareGen runs on camera-free radar and bed/pressure sensors. No CareGen sensor captures images or audio of residents, rooms or conversations.

Data ownership & integrations

Your facility owns its data, always. CareGen integrates with the care systems you already run rather than asking you to replace them, and nothing is shared with a third party without your agreement.

A clinical-risk and compliance service. CareGen supports your clinical team's judgement, it doesn't replace it, and it isn't a diagnostic device.
